# Overview of the files contained in the data folder 

- Raw_Survey_Responses.csv - raw survey responses 
- stigmatised_deprivation_and_loneliness_data_features_used_in_the_paper - Exclusively features used in the paper
- stigmatised_deprivation_and_loneliness_dataset - complete dataset (generated from the raw Survey responses)
- data_dict.csv - Data Dictionary
